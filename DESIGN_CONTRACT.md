## 1. Las ocho condiciones de réplica

A screen is replicated when it satisfies all eight. Verify before closing any task.

1. **Estructura.** Mismo orden y jerarquía de bloques: barra lateral, encabezado, contenido a 1760 px máximo, panel de control.
2. **Medidas.** Bordes de 2 px (2.5 px en tarjetas héroe y barras fijas), radios de la escala 8/10/12/14/16/18/20/22, sombras duras 3/4/5 px sin difuminado.
3. **Color.** Solo tokens del sistema. Ningún color nuevo, ningún degradado, ninguna transparencia salvo el velo de modal.
4. **Tipografía.** Plus Jakarta Sans; títulos en 800 con tracking negativo, etiquetas en mayúsculas 700 con tracking positivo, cuerpo 600.
5. **Copy.** Textos en español (es-MX) copiados literalmente del diseño, incluidos montos y nombres de ejemplo. No se reescriben.
6. **Interacción.** El sello al presionar (2 px + sombra a 1 px, 100 ms) en todo lo tocable; hover solo eleva en escritorio.
7. **Estados.** Cada lista y cada reporte implementan cargando, vacío, error y con datos, más el recorte por rol.
8. **Accesibilidad.** Foco visible amarillo, objetivos de 44 px mínimo, severidad siempre con icono además de color.

## 2. Prohibiciones

- No component library that brings its own styling. Radix supplies behaviour only; every pixel is ours (ADR-057).
- No Tailwind, and no utility framework whose defaults are not the tokens below.
- No hex literal, `rgba()`, radius, border width or font size written inline in a component. `pnpm lint:design` fails on all five.
- No icons outside the Lucide idiom, drawn inline at stroke-width 2.2–2.4 with `currentColor`.
- No emoji in the interface, except a single glyph as the visual in an empty state.
- No gradients, no blur, no soft shadows, no glassmorphism, no dark theme.
- No `gray400` as text. It is a fill; the `*Text` tokens are the legible pairs.
- Money is integer centavos and is formatted only at the boundary, with `@xangarro/domain` (CLAUDE.md §2.8).

## 4. Shape

**Radii** — the strict ladder, nothing else: 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22; plus the dense steps 11 and 13 (ADR-076).

**Off-ladder shapes** (data-viz marks and full pills): `mark` 2, `markLg` 4, `pill` 9999.

**Borders** — only two widths exist: `thin` 2px solid #0D0D0D, `thick` 2.5px solid #0D0D0D.

**Shadows** — hard drops, zero blur, zero alpha:

| Token | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `small` | `3px 3px 0 #0D0D0D` |
| `card` | `4px 4px 0 #0D0D0D` |
| `hero` | `5px 5px 0 #0D0D0D` |
| `pressed` | `1px 1px 0 #0D0D0D` |

## 5. Type

**Family** — `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif`, self-hosted. Anton for the wordmark only.

**Weights** — regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600, bold 700, extraBold 800, black 900. **800 is the portal's maximum**: Google Fonts tops out there and a 900 request renders as synthetic bold (ADR-058 §8).

**Mobile ramp** (`fontSizes`) — 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 48.

**Portal ramp** (`portalFontSizes`) — 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22, 24, 26, 30, 34, 36, 40, 44, 52, 56. Richer than the phone's, per the design handoff. **12 is the floor for both.**

**Emoji as illustration** — md 40, lg 56. Never dragged along by a type-scale change.

**Tracking** — tightest `-0.04em`, tighter `-0.03em`, tight `-0.02em`, normal `0`, wide `0.05em`, wider `0.07em`, widest `0.08em`.

## 6. Motion

- **Press is the only global motion**: `translate(2px, 2px)` with the shadow to `1px 1px 0`, 100 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`.
- **Cards may lift on hover**, `translate(-1px, -1px)` to `6px 6px 0`, 120 ms. That is the one exception.
- Loading is a **static grey block**. Never a shimmer, never a spinner.
- All of it is wrapped in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.

## 7. Verification

1. **Comparación lado a lado** — `pnpm design:compare <pantalla>`. A review, never a CI gate.
2. **Auditoría de valores** — `pnpm lint:design`. A CI gate, ratcheting; the baseline may fall, never rise.
3. **Recorrido de estados y rol** — Playwright over the six states × three roles, with `@axe-core/playwright`.
