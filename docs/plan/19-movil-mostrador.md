# Track M — la caja en el teléfono y la tableta (El Mostrador)

The native app (`apps/mobile` + `packages/ui`) becomes the same register as the
web caja (`apps/web/src/operador/`), in the El Mostrador language
(`docs/design/el-mostrador.md`, ADR-117). The boards are on the design canvas,
page «Teléfono y tableta» (24 phone boards `Mv*`, 3 tablet boards `Tb*`); they
translate the approved web caja boards and are the spec once the owner
approves them.

Owner decisions (2026-09-27):

- The device is for operating, not managing. The phone does exactly what the
  web caja does: no Merma tab (merma is a movement in Inventario), no
  Conversión, Auditoría, nómina, crédito or product management.
- Sales are cancelled with a reason, never edited or deleted from the caja
  (the owner's side of that is M-10).
- Four tabs: Inicio, Cobrar, Ventas, Mi turno; the rest opens from Inicio and
  Mi turno. Web side panels become bottom sheets.
- Tablets are supported: under 760 px the phone tabs; 760 to 1279 px an 88 px
  icon rail with the topbar; from 1280 px the full sidebar.

**19/19 green on the iPad Pro 11 (2026-10-19, final cut):** the last nine fell to four harness contracts, each verified green in its flow. (1) _The FAB park must sit dead in both layouts_ — (40,110) landed on the iPad's 88px rail, (200,200) sat mid-checkout on the wide layout and its taps opened the dev menu mid-sale; the status-bar strip (201,8) is dead in both. (2) _The iPad sheet has no safe keyboard dismissal_ — `hideKeyboard` leaves the OS keyboard up, a title tap lands on the backdrop's close, and a drag swallows into the drag-to-dismiss area the taller phone sheet never shows; so the sheets type settle-first-then-type (waitForAnimationToEnd before inputText, no dismissal), and the one drag that helps (`caja-cierre-discrepancia`) goes before the close tap. (3) _`scrollUntilVisible` swipes the screen, not the sheet_ — on the short iPad sheet a centre-of-screen swipe starts on the scrim, whose tap closes the sheet outright (`gastos-registrar` died twice to this: the title tap, then the chip scroll); the cure is a `swipe` anchored on the sheet's own body (`registrar-gasto-sheet-body`; `scroll` takes no element in Maestro 2.5.1) fired conditionally on `notVisible`, and `gasto-guardar` needs no scroll at all — it lives in the pinned footer. (4) _Mi turno's rows stack past the iPad's fold_ — the `otros-*` assert scrolls first. What remains for the clause is only the full iPhone+iPad regression (`full-regression.sh` on both devices).

**The iPad sweep ran (2026-10-19): 10 of the 19 flows green on the iPad Pro 11 (M5, iOS 26.5)** — activation, smoke-launch, login-lockout, bloqueo, settings-desvincular, gastos-pagar-recurrente, cancelaciones-nav, avisos-responder, cierre-cuadra and caja-abrir-cerrar — with the harness's own form-factor fixes landed to get there: every tab-key in the flows AND shared subflows accepts the tablet chrome (`tab-|rail-|sidebar-`, except lock-caja's deliberately distinct branches), the dev client's floating gear parks mid-content ((40,110) sat exactly on the iPad's 88px left rail and opened the dev menu instead of navigating), and Inicio's phone-only atajos gained a tablet branch that deep-links the same screen (the navigate-to-settings pattern). The kAX wedge recurs mid-sweep and the CoreSimulator killall cures it each time — the sweep driver (recovery + one retry per flow) is the working protocol, but re-priming after each recovery is manual today: the iOS «Open in …?» dialog eats the deep link's url param. The nine that remained at this point (the checkout-surface flows, the two sheet-keyboard flows, cierre-faltante, mi-turno, por-enviar) fell in the final cut below.

The iPad blocker **was the host, not Maestro** (2026-10-15): the same CoreSimulator-service wedge that blinded the iPhone cured with `killall -9 com.apple.CoreSimulator.CoreSimulatorService` (sims shut down) — and with the service fresh, Maestro 2.5.1 drives the iPad: `activation.yaml` ran **green on the iPad Pro 11-inch (M5), iOS 26.5** (device pinned with `MAESTRO_DEVICE_UDID`; the dev menu that meets the first connection closes on its `xmark`). Two facts from the bring-up: the app build **crashes at launch on iOS 27.0** (`___UIApplicationEvaluateRuntimeIssueForNoSceneLifecycleAdoption` — the dev client does not adopt scene lifecycle; 27.0 enforces it, 26.5 does not), so the iPad pass targets 26.5 devices until the client gains scene adoption; and two sims can share a display name across runtimes — always pin the UDID.
