# Track M — la caja en el teléfono y la tableta (El Mostrador)

The native app (`apps/mobile` + `packages/ui`) becomes the same register as the
web caja (`apps/web/src/operador/`), in the El Mostrador language
(`docs/design/el-mostrador.md`, ADR-117). The boards are on the design canvas,
page «Teléfono y tableta» (24 phone boards `Mv*`, 3 tablet boards `Tb*`); they
translate the approved web caja boards and are the spec once the owner
approves them.

Owner decisions (2026-09-27):

- The device is for operating, not managing. The phone does exactly what the
  web caja does: no Merma tab (merma is a movement in Inventario), no
  Conversión, Auditoría, nómina, crédito or product management.
- Sales are cancelled with a reason, never edited or deleted from the caja
  (the owner's side of that is M-10).
- Four tabs: Inicio, Cobrar, Ventas, Mi turno; the rest opens from Inicio and
  Mi turno. Web side panels become bottom sheets.
- Tablets are supported: under 760 px the phone tabs; 760 to 1279 px an 88 px
  icon rail with the topbar; from 1280 px the full sidebar.

The «checkout tap anomaly» closed as **two driver contracts, not hit-testing**: (1) _a subflow's own `env:` shadows the caller's `runFlow` env_ — `cobrar-ticket.yaml` carried `METODO: Efectivo` up front, so every method tap looked right while the subflow read the default (proven by the DB row: ticket charged as Transferencia once the env moved to the callers); (2) _an explicit `aria-label` folds a Pressable's children out of the iOS a11y tree_ — ventas rows, fiado client radios and the abono quick chips must be matched by their labels/ids (`Ver venta V-0001, $27.50, Transferencia`, `venta-fila-V-0001`, `fiado-cliente-<ulid>`, «Todo, $35.00»), never by the folded-away texts; the venta row's label now names the metodo and the client so VoiceOver hears them too. Two real app bugs the flows surfaced, both fixed with tests: the Ventas list froze after a cancellation (`marcas` held a stale row snapshot instead of merging folio→motivo marks over fresh data — a venta captured after a cancel never reached the list), and a sale never invalidated the `ventas-turno` query (`teclasAlCobrar` added; TOCADAS matches by the key's first word and cannot see the `caja`-prefixed family). por-enviar's refusal hits **both** rows that name the product (the sale and its stock movement), so its retry is the panel's «Reintentar todos», not one card's button. Not yet done: the full iPhone+iPad regression, and the legacy pre-activation flows (~12, `demo`/`wizard` buckets) plus the stale shared subflows (authenticate-director/operativo, complete-first-run, director-setup, first-run-onboarding, open-director-tools, select-operativo) still to rework or delete — A-16's ledger.

**The iPad sweep ran (2026-10-19): 10 of the 19 flows green on the iPad Pro 11 (M5, iOS 26.5)** — activation, smoke-launch, login-lockout, bloqueo, settings-desvincular, gastos-pagar-recurrente, cancelaciones-nav, avisos-responder, cierre-cuadra and caja-abrir-cerrar — with the harness's own form-factor fixes landed to get there: every tab-key in the flows AND shared subflows accepts the tablet chrome (`tab-|rail-|sidebar-`, except lock-caja's deliberately distinct branches), the dev client's floating gear parks mid-content ((40,110) sat exactly on the iPad's 88px left rail and opened the dev menu instead of navigating), and Inicio's phone-only atajos gained a tablet branch that deep-links the same screen (the navigate-to-settings pattern). The kAX wedge recurs mid-sweep and the CoreSimulator killall cures it each time — the sweep driver (recovery + one retry per flow) is the working protocol, but re-priming after each recovery is manual today: the iOS «Open in …?» dialog eats the deep link's url param. What remains for the clause, by cause: the checkout-surface flows (venta-detalle-cancelar, ventas-lista, fiado-abono-recordar — the ticket bar and checkout ids sit differently on the wide layout), the two sheet-keyboard flows (gastos-registrar carries the drag fix; caja-cierre-discrepancia's «cierre-hecho» assert), cierre-faltante, mi-turno and por-enviar (bring-up races after recovery), all needing a fresh session with the manual prime in the loop.

The iPad blocker **was the host, not Maestro** (2026-10-15): the same CoreSimulator-service wedge that blinded the iPhone cured with `killall -9 com.apple.CoreSimulator.CoreSimulatorService` (sims shut down) — and with the service fresh, Maestro 2.5.1 drives the iPad: `activation.yaml` ran **green on the iPad Pro 11-inch (M5), iOS 26.5** (device pinned with `MAESTRO_DEVICE_UDID`; the dev menu that meets the first connection closes on its `xmark`). Two facts from the bring-up: the app build **crashes at launch on iOS 27.0** (`___UIApplicationEvaluateRuntimeIssueForNoSceneLifecycleAdoption` — the dev client does not adopt scene lifecycle; 27.0 enforces it, 26.5 does not), so the iPad pass targets 26.5 devices until the client gains scene adoption; and two sims can share a display name across runtimes — always pin the UDID. What remains for the clause: the 14 activated flows across the iPad (bring-up races on re-activation are the current rough edge), then the full-regression clause.
